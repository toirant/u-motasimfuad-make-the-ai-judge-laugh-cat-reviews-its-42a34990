# Make the AI Judge Laugh: Cat Reviews Its Human

A [make.fun](https://make.fun/bounty/u-motasimfuad-make-the-ai-judge-laugh-cat-reviews-its-42a34990) bounty entry: one funny single-line one-star review written by a house cat about its human, under 200 characters, judged head to head by an AI judge in three private blind rounds.

## The entry

> One star. He bought a $400 cat tree, then claimed it for himself. I sleep in the box it came in, and somehow I'm the weird one.

127 characters, one line, deadpan review shape with a sting at the end: the human bought the cat a luxury tree, stole it for himself, and the cat — sleeping in the cardboard box, as cats do — is somehow the weird one.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```

`main.ts` writes the review to `/output/entry.txt` (or `$OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` when set). Plain Node, no dependencies, no network, deterministic.
